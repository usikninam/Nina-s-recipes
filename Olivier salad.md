---
- [ ] 230g canned peas
- [ ] 300g JBB parówki tęczowe
- [ ] 100g pickled cucumbers (augurken)
- [ ] 4 boiled eggs
- [ ] 140g carrot
- [ ] 270g potato
- [ ] 70g mayonaise

---
1. Carrot and potato peel and cut in cubes. Boil until ready (±10min). Cool down.
2. Drain peas and cut the rest into cubes
3. Mix everything together
