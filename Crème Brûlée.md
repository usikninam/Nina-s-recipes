4 white forms exactly:

- [ ] 6 yolks
- [ ] 600 ml 30% śmietanka do zupy lub ubijania
- [ ] 2 vanilla stick
- [ ] 80g sugar (+for top)

---

1. Preheat the oven to 135ºC.
2. Add heavy cream to a small saucepan.
3. On a cutting board, slice open the vanilla beans and scrape the seeds out.
4. Bring cream and the vanilla bean and its scrapings to a simmer over low heat. Remove from heat and let stand for 15 minutes.
5. In a bowl, separate the egg yolks from the egg whites.
6. Whisk together the egg yolks, sugar, and salt.
7. Strain the cream mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a bowl. Discard any solids and the vanilla bean pod.
8. Slowly whisk the hot cream into the egg yolks.
9. Place the ramekins(baking forms) in a baking dish and pour very hot water into the baking dish until it fills the dish by about a third.
10. Divide custard among ramekins. Bake for 40min (custard edges have set, but the centre wobbles slightly).
11. Remove from the oven and let cool completely before chilling for several hours or up to 2 days.
12. When ready to serve, sprinkle 1 tsp of sugar over the top of each chilled custard.
13. Using a blow torch, caramelise the sugar until dark amber and bubbling. Serve the crème brûlée immediately.
