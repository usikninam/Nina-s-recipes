lisbon sauce:
- [ ] 1 bell pepper
- [ ] 4 cloves of garlic
- [ ] ½ cup olive oil
- [ ] 1 tsp salt

base:
- [ ] 4 tomatoes
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 2 cloves of garlic
- [ ] 2 bay leaves
- [ ] parsley
- [ ] 1 pinch of chili flakes
- [ ] olive oil
- [ ] salt
- [ ] broth (lobster/fish/clam juice)

seafood:
- [ ] mussels
- [ ] clams
- [ ] squid
- [ ] scallops
- [ ] cod
- [ ] sea bass
- [ ] shrimp

- [ ] 3 cloves of garlic
- [ ] olive oil
- [ ] 1/4 cup canned tomato paste
- [ ] 1/2 cup white wine
- [ ] lemon
- [ ] parsley

1. Lisbon sauce:
    1. Bake bell pepper in the oven until dark skin, wrap in foil to steam, clean.
    2. Put all the ingredients (pepper, garlic, olive oil, salt) in a blender and grind to a homogeneous consistency.
2. Base:
    1. Fry finely chopped tomatoes in olive oil, add onion (half rings), bay leaf, parsley, garlic, chilli flakes, stew it for 5 min.
    2. Add the broth, evaporate at high temperature for 8 minutes.
3. In a cataplana:
    1. Sauté the garlic in olive oil.
    2. Put clams and mussels first.
    3. Put squids and scallops on top.
    4. Pour Lisbon sauce, wine and base.
    5. Put fish on top and sprinkle with parsley.
    6. Close the cataplana and cook for 10 minutes.
    7. Open, put peeled shrimp and lemon sliced into 8 parts. Close and cook another 5 minutes.
