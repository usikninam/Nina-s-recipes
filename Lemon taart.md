Filling:
- [ ] 3 egg yolks (large)
- [ ] 1 whole large egg
- [ ] ~~20g cornstarch~~
- [ ] ½ tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon zest
- [ ] ±180ml lemon juice (3-4 lemons)
- [ ] 50g sugar
- [ ] 65g cold unsalted cubed butter

Filling:
1. Place a saucepan with 2-inches of water on medium-low heat. You will need a heat-proof bowl that can be set on the saucepan.
2. In the bowl over the saucepan, add the egg yolks, egg, salt, cornstarch, and sugar. Don’t place it over the saucepan yet.
3. Whisk until light, and fluffy. The sugar should almost melt. Gradually, add the lemon juice and lemon zest.
4. Place the bowl over the saucepan and continue to whisk as the mixture thickens. The mixture should cook gently with the steam from the water in the saucepan below.
5. Continue to stir and cook until it thickens enough to coats the back of your wooden spoon. About 10 to 15 minutes Depending on the heat it can take up to 20 minutes. Remove from heat. (_If the curd is too thin, it will not set when chilled in the tart. So, make sure the curd is thick and coats the back of the spoon thickly_.)
6. Gradually, add cubes of chilled butter one at a time. Make sure each piece is well incorporated. The mixture will continue to thicken as it cools.
7. Once all the butter is incorporated, strain it through a mesh or a strainer. (_It is essential to strain the curd so you remove the zest as well as any cooked eggs. This way the tart will be smooth and creamy_.)
8. Pour filling in the lemon curd into the baked tart shell, bake together, until when you shake it wobbles only in the very middle.
9. Cool down and let chill in the fridge for a few hours.
