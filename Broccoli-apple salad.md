350 g broccoli
1 apple
65 g walnuts
25 g carrots
40 g dried cranberries (or golden raisins)
40 g red onion

**Dressing:**
214 g greek yogurt
77 g mayonnaise
1 ½ tbsp apple cider vinegar
3 tsp honey
salt

1. In a medium mixing bowl whisk together Greek yogurt, mayonnaise, vinegar, honey and season with salt to taste (about 1/4 tsp). Chill until ready to use.
2. In a salad bowl toss together broccoli, apples, walnuts, carrots, raisins or cranberries and red onion. Pour in dressing and toss until evenly coated.
