- [ ] 1,5 kg side of salmon , skin on, scaled, pin-boned, from sustainable sources
- [ ] 2 raw beets
- [ ] zest of 1 orange
- [ ] 75g sugar
- [ ] 150 g salt
- [ ] 2 tsp coriander
- [ ] 60g of fresh dill

1. Mix your coriander with salt and sugar.
2. Add orange zest and some black pepper.
3. Add grated beetroot.
4. Take salmon deboned fillet and put on the tray. Then, place the beetroot mixture on the top of it. Spread the beetroot over the salmon fillet.
5. Next, cover your beetroot salmon with film. Take another tray and press over your salmon. Put something heavy on the tray. Next, place your salmon into the fridge to cure for at least 8 hours or even better overnight (24h also great).
6. Next, unveil your incredible beetroot cured salmon. Give your salmon a little rinse in cold water. Dry your salmon with a paper towel. Slice your salmon into thin strips.
