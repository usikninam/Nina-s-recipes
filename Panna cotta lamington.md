Per piece (1/8): ~1,174 kcal • 13.2 g protein • 80.5 g fat • 102.3 g carbs

### Sponge (deep 30x20cm baking sheet)

- [ ] 5 eggs
- [ ] 125 g sugar
- [ ] 165 g self-raising flour
- [ ] 25 g unsalted butter, melted

---

### Panna cotta layer

- [ ] 2 titanium-strength gelatine leaves (= 2½ Dr. Oetker)
- [ ] 800 ml pure cream (35%, slaagroom, too heavy dessert, use lighter)
- [ ] 80 g sugar
- [ ] 2 tsp vanilla extract

---

### Berry compote

- [ ] 250 g mixed frozen berries
- [ ] 100 g caster sugar
- [ ] zest of 1 orange

---

### Chocolate coating

- [ ] 250 g dark chocolate (>= 55%)
- [ ] 100 ml milk
- [ ] 50 g unsalted butter
- [ ] 100 g icing sugar

---

### To coat

- [ ] 100 g desiccated coconut
- [ ] 100 g long-thread coconut
- [ ] 100 g chipped coconut

---

### Day 1

#### Sponge cake

1. Preheat oven to 180ºC.
2. Eggs and sugar whisk on high speed to double in volume.
3. Mix flour in with spatula in 3 steps.
4. Add melted butter and gently mix it in.
5. Prepare baking sheet with parchment.
6. Bake >= 15min, DON'T open the oven before 15min pass.
7. Cool completely in the baking sheet (don't take out)

#### Panna cotta

1. Soak gelatine in cold-cold water.
2. Combine cream, sugar and vanilla in sauce pan over medium heat, continuously steering bring almost to boil (This will prevent the fat from separating from the cream and will ensure the panna cotta remains white.) Take off the heat.
3. Squeeze out the gelatine leaves and add to cream and mix to dissolve.
4. Set aside to cool down, mix from time to time (skin forming on top is ok, just stir it now and then).

#### Soaking the sponge

1. Leave sponge in the thin and make holes (tightly next to each other) with a fork.
2. Pure panna cotta over the sponge to soak it up evenly (pay attention to corners)
3. Put in the fridge to set over night (uncovered)

#### Berry compote

1. All ingredients in saucepan over medium heat.
2. When starts to boil reduce heat and simmer for 15min. (You can do it longer if you don't want whole fruits but more jam like texture)
3. Pour into a bowl and cover with film, store in the fridge until assembling lamington.

---

### Day 2

#### Prepare peaces

1. Remove sponge cake with panna cotta from baking sheet and cut it in peaces.
2. Cut (vertically, 8 peaces) in portions and assemble 2 peaces with jam in between, dark side of the sponge goes in the middle of the lamington sandwich.
   I tried to cut sponge cake half, put jam and then cut in portions. I'd rather cut peaces first and then assemble them with berry compote in the middle.

#### Chocolate coating

1. Combine all in heatproof bowl and set over pan with boiling water to melt and mix all together (+- 10min). Stir occasionally.

#### Coating

1. Prepare plate with mixed coconut board with baking sheet or plastic wrap sprincled with coconut, bowl/plate with chocolate and take your lamington peaces from the fridge
2. Coat peaces in the chocolate, let it set a bit few min (don't leave for too long, so that coconut still sticks to it)
3. Coat with coconut and let it rest in the fridge for at least 1h.

#### Note

The lamingtons may be stored in the fridge for up to 4 days.
They travel well in a cooked bag with extra coconut for padding.
