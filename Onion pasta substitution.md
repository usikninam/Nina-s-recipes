---

- [ ] 500g onion
- [ ] 10 cloves of garlic (~30g)
- [ ] 15g sunflower oil
- [ ] 15g sun-dried tomatoes
- [ ] 200ml coconut milk
- [ ] 1 tomato
- [ ] 100g spinach
- [ ] oregano
- [ ] paprika
- [ ] garlic powder
- [ ] salt
- [ ] pepper

---

1. Slice onions and put them in a baking dish. Season with oregano, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
2. Add sun-dried tomatoes and sunflower oil. Mix well.
3. Put the garlic cloves in the centre. Cover and bake at 190 °C for 1 hour.
4. Stir in coconut milk, chopped tomato and spinach.
5. Mix and bake a bit more until creamy.
