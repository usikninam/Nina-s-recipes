for 16 - 20 scones
- [ ] 450g self raising flour
- [ ] 113g butter
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 180g whole milk (if with water +120g flour)

serve with:
- [ ] whipped cream
- [ ] raspberry jam

---
1. Preheat oven to 200ºC.
2. Prepare the dough: rub butter with self raising flour, mix egg and milk. Mix together dry and wet.
3. Roll out on floured surface 1,5cm thick.
4. Cut out circles.
5. Cake for 15 minutes in hot over.
6. When baked cut halfs horisontaly and enjoy with fresh whipped cream and raspberry jam.
