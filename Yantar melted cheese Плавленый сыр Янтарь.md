- [ ] 450g Mazurski Smak twaróg, full fat (can be lean)
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] pinch of citric acid (or lemon juice)
- [ ] 1 tsp baking soda
- [ ] oregano
- [ ] black pepper
- [ ] salt, to taste

![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/8082AD7F-88B7-458D-B467-D84629F9214F_1_105_c.jpeg>)

---

1. Use a non-stick pan (thick-bottomed is even better). Set to very low heat (about 3 out of 10 on induction) so the egg does not scramble.
2. Add the twaróg and the egg straight away, while the pan is still cold. Season with salt, oregano and black pepper. Add a pinch of citric acid. Mix well and keep stirring for about 2 minutes as it warms up.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/3FB53FC8-2812-46DC-B6E9-22ADEADA371B_1_105_c.jpeg>)
3. After it has sat on the heat for a couple of minutes, stir in 1 tsp baking soda. It will foam and bubble a little — that is normal.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/FE64E7DD-DFA6-45B6-9272-2D9C3573FD39_1_105_c.jpeg>)
4. Keep stirring constantly for about 6–7 minutes, until the mixture is thicker than pancake batter and drips in a stretchy ribbon. Then it is ready.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/51D70773-B8FB-4BC6-950A-01DAB61BB41B_1_105_c.jpeg>)
5. Pour into a container. Stir in extra oregano if you like. Chill in the fridge until set.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/207FC2F8-1B7F-4FC3-BB16-AA3AD0A7EA55_1_105_c.jpeg>)

Spread on bread, or use warm as a sauce (for example over pasta).
