- [ ] 3 medium eggs
- [ ] 80g caster sugar
- [ ] 110g plain flour
- [ ] 75g melted unsalted butter
- [ ] 2 tbsp poppy seeds
- [ ] 2g baking powder
- [ ] one lemon zest

---

1. Whisk the eggs and sugar.
2. Slowly slip the cooled butter down the side of the bowl and fold in very gently and carefully.
3. Add all dry ingredients.
4. To bake the Madeleines, heat the oven to 170C. Generously butter, then flour, the Madeleines tins. Spoon the batter into the tins and bake for 15-17 minutes.
5. When the Madeleines are golden and baked all the way through, remove from the oven and leave to cool for 2 minutes.
