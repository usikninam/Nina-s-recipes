---
title: Lentil - sweet potato dish
---

- [ ] 200g yellow lentils
- [ ] 1 medium sweet potato
- [ ] green onions
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves
- [ ] 250ml non-dairy milk
- [ ] 1tsp baking powder

- [ ] 1/2tsp Korean chilli flakes (Gochugaru)
- [ ] 1/2tsp Italian herbs Season
- [ ] 1/2tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1/4tsp turmeric powder
- [ ] 1/2tsp onion powder

- [ ] (optional) Tomato sauce
- [ ] (optional) Chopped parsley

---

1. Rinse 200g of yellow lentils in cold water.
2. Add cold water and soak lentils for 3 hours.
3. Peel and julienne or grate 1 medium sweet potato.
4. Add oil in a pan.
5. Add grated sweet potato.
6. Chop 3-4 green onions. Add green onions to the pan.
7. Mince 2 garlic cloves. Add minced garlic to the pan.
8. Season with korean chilli flakes, italian herbs, pinch of salt. Mix well and cook for 2-3 minutes. 
9. Place cooked sweet potatoes to a large mixing bowl.
10. Rinse soaked lentils with water and drain. Place lentils to a blender bowl.
11. Season lentils with garlic powder, turmeric powder, onion powder, salt.
12. Add 250ml of dairy-free milk.
13. Blend until smooth.
14. Add 1tsp baking powder. Blend again.
15. Add lentils mixture to the grated sweet potatoes and mix well.
16. Pour the lentils mixture into a lightly oiled skillet.
17. Put the lid on and cook over medium heat for 5-7 minutes.
18. Flip over and cook over medium heat for another 5-7 minutes or until golden brown.

* Serve this delicious lentils and sweet potato dish with tomato sauce, salsa, dairy-free yogurt or the dip sauce you enjoy the most, chopped parsley.
