Yield: ~15psc

---

### Zefir

- [ ] ~350 g apples (for 200 g apple puree)
- [ ] 100g water
- [ ] 8g agar-agar
- [ ] 300 g sugar (150 g in the syrup, 150 g with the egg whites)
- [ ] 2 egg whites
- [ ] (optional) 10g icing sugar, for dusting

**Note:** without chocolate it has the taste and texture of apples — to be like zefir the recipe has to be adjusted, but still a nice dessert. Try next time to peel and stew the apples and boil them for a while. [Try like this too](https://youtube.com/shorts/62oWv-PFiQE).

### Glaze

- [ ] 100 g chocolate
- [ ] 4 g oil

---

1. Prepare a piping bag in advance - once the mass thickens there is no time.
2. Bake, microwave, or steam the apples until soft. Blend to a smooth puree and weigh **200 g**.
3. For the syrup: mix water, agar-agar and the puree (and 150 g of the sugar). Put on the heat.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/IMG_9967.jpeg>)
4. As soon as the syrup boils, start whipping the egg whites, adding the remaining 150 g sugar little by little and whip about 4–5 minutes. Keep the syrup bubbling while whipping (at least 2min for agar-agar activation).
5. Take the apple syrup off the heat and straight away add it in small portions while whipping. Continue about 3 minutes more. The mass thickens very fast.
6. Pipe onto parchment immediately. Leave to dry at room temperature for 2–3 hours.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/IMG_9968.jpeg>)
7. Dust lightly with icing sugar (but you can skip they are barely sticky).
8. Temper 70 g chocolate, add 3 g oil. Spread over the zefir with a brush. Let the glaze set.
