- [ ] Fresh salmon fillet 500g
- [ ] Smoked salmon 200 g
- [ ] Natural yoghurt 50 ml
- [ ] Sour cream 80 g
- [ ] Juice from 1/2 lemon
- [ ] Fresh dill
- [ ] White ground pepper
- [ ] Pepper ground black
- [ ] Salt 2 pinch

Stock:
- [ ] White dry wine 150 ml
- [ ] Bay leaf 3 pcs.
- [ ] Onion 1
- [ ] Fresh parsley
- [ ] Water 1l

---

1. Prepare stock for salmon: pour wine, water, put bay leaf, parsley (whole), onion (cutted in half) in a pan. Cook for 5 minutes.
2. Pieces of fresh salmon put in boiling stock and cook for 10 minutes (the broth should not boil strongly), then cool it in the stock.
3. While the salmon is cooling, squeeze juice from 1/2 lemon and chop dill. Chop the smoked salmon.
4. Cut boiled salmon, previously removing the skin from it. Put the boiled fish in a bowl and knead it well with a fork to make something like mashed potatoes.
5. Add the rest of the ingredients: smoked salmon, sour cream, lemon juice, salt, pepper, dill and yogurt. Mix well. If necessary, add salt, pepper, lemon juice to taste.
6. Salmon rillettes is ready, it needs to stay in the fridge for few hours. In fact, it becomes even tastier and piquant after standing for 1 day in the refrigerator.
