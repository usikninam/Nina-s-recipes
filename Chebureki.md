Mom's recipe (/img/CheburekiCookbookPage.png) · for 12psc · 1 cheburek ±= 419 kcal, 16.7 g protein

**Dough:**

step 1:
- [ ] 320g flour
- [ ] 500ml boiling water


step 2:
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 2 tbsp (=28g) sunflour oil 
- [ ] 320g flour

**Filling:**

- [ ] 750g meanced meat (18% fat) 50/50 pork/beef
- [ ] 375g onion (shreded on star grater)
- [ ] salt
- [ ] pepper

**Frying:**

- [ ] 56g sunflour oil (you need more, but ± this amount will be absorbed by chebureki)

1. Dough step 1: mix flour and boiling water with spoon.
2. Let it cool down
3. Dough step 2: add egg, oil and with the remaining flour (leave a 2 tbsp for rolling) knit the dough. Cover and set aside (just to mix filling)
![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/IMG_9225.png>)
4. Prepare filling: mix meanced meat with shreaded onion, salt and pepper.
5. Devide dough to 12 even parts.
6. Roll thin circle (other parts keep covered to prevent from drying).
![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/IMG_9227.png>)
![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/IMG_9226.png>)
7. Put filling on one half leaving 1cm free of filling on the edge. Pinch it tightly, don't leave air inside, make sure there is no escape for 'juice' from the inside!
5. Fry in a lot of well preheated oil (not deepfried but close to it) few min on each side.
6. Eat hot.

<sub>You can freeze this dough. You can leave dough and prepared filling in the refregerator for a day. </sub>
