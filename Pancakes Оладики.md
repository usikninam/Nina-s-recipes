### Kwark / Greek Yogurt + cherries version

± 8-12 peices

- [ ] 190 g all-purpose flour
- [ ] 8 g baking powder
- [ ] 2 g baking soda
- [ ] 40 g sugar
- [ ] pinch of salt
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 230 g Magere Franse kwark / 230g 10% Greek yogurt
- [ ] a few drops of lemon juice
- [ ] a few drops of vanilla extract
- [ ] 35 g oil
- [ ] - / 250g well drained cherry (Freshona Kersen op siroop) // TODO: try with frozen cherries

1. In one bowl, mix the dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, baking soda, sugar, salt).
2. In another, whisk the wet ingredients (eggs, kwark, lemon juice, oil).
3. Pour the wet into the dry and stir until just combined.
4. Cook over medium heat with the lid on, about 2 minutes per side.

(you can freeze them (portion straight away - they will freeze to each other), to serve - microwave for a few min)
