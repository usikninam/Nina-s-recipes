Full recipe (4 portions)

Dough:

- [ ] 300 ml kefir
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar
- [ ] 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- [ ] 570 g flour (450 g dough + 120 g to dust when rolling the khachapuri)
- [ ] 10 g baking powder

Filling:

(Note: drain cheese well. Tried to make with gouda and it is too dry, not salty enough and too thick.)

- [ ] 264 g feta
- [ ] 332 g mozzarella

---

1. Drain the cheeses well.
2. Mix kefir, egg, salt, sugar and oil. Add 450 g flour mixed with baking powder and knead until soft(very sticky). Rest ~20 min.
3. Mix feta and mozzarella. Divide filling into 4.
4. Divide dough into 4. Flatten one piece, put filling in the middle, pinch the dough closed over it. Flip, then roll out, using ~30 g extra flour per piece. Watch for holes — cheese leaks if the dough tears.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/8ED3F852-6D33-488C-9AAB-5614078ECBAF_1_105_c.jpeg>)
5. Cook in a dry pan over medium heat ~3–4 min per side, until browned in patches.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/D7A23A92-8F6E-4A50-86A7-F3D49F1C1265_1_105_c.jpeg>)
6. Serve warm. Mom's recommendation: Brush with butter after frying.
