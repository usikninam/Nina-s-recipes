- [ ] 600g potatoes
- [ ] 200g semolina flour (for sweet potato more flour is needed not 3:1, but 2,5:1 e.g. 100g sweet potato 40g semola)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt

---

1. Steam potatoes then peel it. (For sweet potato version bake it, cut in halfs and scrape inside)
2. Mush the potatoes and add flour and salt. knead a little bit.
3. Cut the dough into small pieces and shape them with gnocchi board.
4. Boil for 1 - 2 minutes.
