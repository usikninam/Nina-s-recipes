- [ ] 500 g flower
- [ ] 350 g purple sweet potato
- [ ] 10 g salt (2% of the flower)
- [ ] 240 ml room temperature water
- [ ] 10 g fresh yeast (*4 grams of active dry yeast or 3 grams of instant dry yeast)
- [ ] 45g butter
- [ ] 16g garlic
- [ ] parsley
- [ ] koriander

1. Boil sweet potato 20-30 min
2. Pill and mash potato
3. Mix yeast with water in big bowl
4. Add sweet potato to a bowl
5. Add salt and slowly add flour
6. Knit the dough for 20 min
7. Let it rise for 2-3 hours in bowl cowered with film
8. Split it in 5 peaces and make balls out of it.
9. Cower with wet towel (keep the towel wet that dough does not stick to it) for 30 min.
10. Cut garlic, parsley and koriander, mix with softened butter
11. Make ball flat (1-2 cm) and fry on pen cowered with flour 3-4 min on each side
12. Let it cool down before cutting
13. Cut half (like bun for burger) and fry cut side on dry pen.
14. Spread butter mix on hot bread and fold it with the other toasted and buttered side.
