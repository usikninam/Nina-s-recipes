White:
150g flour
75ml boiling water
½tsp salt
10-11g olive oil

Green spinach dough:
150g flour
75ml spinach juice
½tsp salt
10-11g olive oil

For one portion of white dough to make ==19 dumplings with potato== you need exactly:
300g of potato
1 large onion (2/3 with potato for filling, 1/3 for topping)
10g olive oil to fry onion
