White/ Green dough:
- [ ] 150g flour
- [ ] 75ml boiling water/ fresh cold spinach juice
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 10 g olive oil

1. Mix everything together
2. Let it rest for 15 min. Meanwhile prepare the filling.
3. Split the dough in a few peaces. Take one and roll it into a 'sausage' roughly 4cm diameter. Keep the rest covered to prevent druing the surface out.
4. Cut 'sausage' into peaces 1cm wide.
5. Roll each into a circle, put filling inside nad pinch into dumpling.
Boiling:
6. Put a lot of water to boil. Salt it.
7. Swirl water with the spoon and one by one drop dumplings in the water, mix it gently (prevents from sticking to the bottom and each other)
8. Boil for 4min form the moment they surfaced.
9. Serve with sourcream/ vinegar/ fried onion/ salt and pepper.

---
<h2>Potato filling:</h2>

<sub>(1 portion of dough = 19 dumplings)</sub>
- [ ] 300g of potato
- [ ] 1 large onion (2/3 with potato for filling, 1/3 for topping)
- [ ] 10g olive oil to fry onion
