---

- [ ] 800 g chicken quarters (or ~1.1 kg bone-in kippenbouten, Note: Chicken fillet turns too dry.)

Dry brine:

- [ ] 10 g salt
- [ ] 5 g sugar

Piri piri marinade:
(1/4 of initial recipe, you can make it and use in few days keep in refregirator)

- [ ] 38 g onion
- [ ] 63 g bell pepper
- [ ] 8 g garlic
- [ ] 4 g Thai bird-eye chilies (1 LIDL rode peper, you can easily double)
- [ ] 2.5 g smoked paprika
- [ ] 4 g salt
- [ ] 8 g sugar
- [ ] 50 g olive oil
- [ ] zest of 1 lemon
- [ ] 12 g lemon juice (±1/4 lemon)
- [ ] 5 g white vinegar

---

1. Mix dry-brine salt and sugar, dust the chicken, rest ~25 min.
2. Blend the marinade. Coat the chicken, refrigerate overnight.
   ![](<{{ site.baseurl }}/img/9B536688-5412-40F8-8482-C89D26F0DAC4_1_105_c.jpeg>)
3. Roast 1h at 140 °C until nearly done .
4. Brush with more marinade, roast at 245 °C 10–15 min until caramelized.
5. Simmer leftover(if any) marinade ~10 min to use as a sauce.
