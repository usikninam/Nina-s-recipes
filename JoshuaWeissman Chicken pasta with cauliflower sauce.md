---

- [ ] chicken breast
- [ ] pasta
- [ ] 1 whole cauliflower
- [ ] 22g parmigiano reggiano
- [ ] 22g pecorino
- [ ] 3 cloves of garlic
- [ ] 125g skim milk

---

1. Overcook cauliflower.
2. Blend together cauliflower, parmigiano reggiano, pecorino, garlic, salt and milk.
3. Fry chicken with salt and pepper.
4. Boil pasta.
5. Mix boiled pasta with cauliflower sauce.
6. Serve with cut chicken and graded parmigiano.
