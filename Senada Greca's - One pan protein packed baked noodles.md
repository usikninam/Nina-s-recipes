2 large portions:

precooked chicken (500g of raw kipdijjfilet)
250g rice noodles
400g coconut milk
100g florets
50g spinach leaves
50g carrot
1 tbsp chives
1 tsp garlic salt
1 tbsp ginger powder
1 tbsp soy sauce (coconut aminos (or soy)
dash chili flakes
360ml of water

---

1. Cut veggies, put everything in pan, cover with lid and cook until tender 190ºC 35min.
2. Mix and serve.
